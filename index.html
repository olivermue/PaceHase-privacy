PaceHase — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026 · Last updated: 1 June 2026

PaceHase is a native Android running-coach app that guides your runs with spoken cues, records your track, and keeps a local history of your runs. This policy explains what data the app processes and why. PaceHase has no user accounts on our servers and does not run analytics or advertising.

Connecting Strava and uploading runs

Connecting Strava is entirely optional. If you choose to connect:

  • Sign-in: to complete Strava's OAuth sign-in without embedding confidential app credentials in the app, the short-lived authorization code and token-refresh requests are relayed through a small server operated by the developer. This server only exchanges authentication tokens with Strava — your runs, location, and track data never pass through it.
  • Uploading a run: when you upload a run, the activity file — which includes its GPS track — is sent directly from your device to Strava under your Strava account. Strava then processes that data under Strava's Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect Strava in the app at any time (this deletes the stored tokens), and revoke access entirely at strava.com/settings/apps.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No user accounts on our servers; no profile is created about you.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

Data sharing

The only third party that ever receives your data is Strava, only if you connect it, and only the activity you choose to upload. No other sharing takes place.

Retention and deletion

Your runs live on your device until you delete them. You can delete an individual run in the app, disconnect Strava to remove stored tokens, or uninstall the app to remove all PaceHase data from your device. Activities you already uploaded to Strava remain on Strava until you delete them there.

Children

PaceHase is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because PaceHase stores your data on your own device under your control, you can access and erase it directly in the app. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at the address above. Where the GDPR applies, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
